Bundle Tag Log

Each photo of a tag adds the next line. Your list is saved on this phone, so you can close the app and keep adding later.

Settings

One-time setup. Reading tags uses a free Google key.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save.

Photos are sent to Google to be read. The key stays on this phone.

Install on your phone. Android (Chrome): menu, then Install app. iPhone (Safari): Share, then Add to Home Screen.
